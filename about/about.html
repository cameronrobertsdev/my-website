Cameron Roberts

Cameron Roberts is an amateur software developer, game dev, and tinkerer currently residing in eastern Washington state in the US.

Cameron was born in Washington and has lived in many states in the western US including Idaho, Oregon, Washington, and Kansas. He developed an interest in computers and game development at a young age and began writing computer games in junior high school.

Cameron holds a Bachelor's Degree in Media Arts and enjoys writing games in C, modding classic games like Quake, and exploring the serene beauty of the rugged Pacific Northwest.
